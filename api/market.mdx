---
title: "Marketplace API"
description: "Optional hosted escrow intents, listings and settlement history."
---

These routes apply **when the marketplace is enabled on a deployment**. Disabled deployments return `409 market_disabled`. Marketplace money is held by an operator-controlled account, not the Allocator claim-fee contract.

## Listings and intent

```http
GET /v1/market/listings
POST /v1/market/listings/{id}/intent
Content-Type: application/json

{ "account": "G...buyer-account..." }
```

Listings are bounded to 100 and include namespace, node, XLM price, fee basis points and escrow account. Read actual amounts rather than hard-coding examples. The buyer must be a valid classic account; the intent route is rate-limited.

An intent supplies its ID/expiry and exact payment instructions: native asset, amount, escrow destination and unique hash memo. Intents are idempotent for the same listing/buyer until expiration; a no-longer-active listing fails. A transient `intent_conflict` may require retrying the intent request, not paying twice.

Before sending money, verify a current Registry namespace transfer proposal from the seller to the buyer. Send the full amount in one payment from a self-custody account and retain the intent memo. The buyer then accepts the namespace transfer with its own authorization.

## Settlement and refunds

Successful verified delivery releases sale proceeds minus the displayed fee and returns an eligible bond. Seller non-delivery during a funded sale can refund the buyer and slash the bond under the rules in the [marketplace guide](/platform/marketplace). Buyer non-acceptance of a standing proposal is handled separately.

Underpayments and overpayment surpluses follow refund rules rather than accumulating into an arbitrary new price. Refund destinations are the sending accounts. Small or uncertain payouts may be parked for operator review. Do not promise guaranteed automatic refund timing or that operator custody removes risk.

## Sale history

```http
GET /v1/market/history/{node}
```

`node` is the namespace's 64-character hex namehash. The endpoint returns up to 50 recent hosted settlement records with namespace, price, seller, buyer, settlement time and badge identifier. Wash-flagged sales are excluded. This is informational hosted provenance, not an on-chain certificate or complete global sale history.

Selling/listing uses authenticated owner-only console flows. Registry ownership and current transfer state remain authoritative.
