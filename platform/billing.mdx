---
title: "Optional managed billing"
description: "Hosted subscriptions are separate from namespace escrow and ownership expiry."
---

Billing is an optional hosted feature **when enabled on a deployment**. It does not add a mandatory annual Soran ownership fee to Registry, Registrar or Resolver.

## Three separate costs

| Cost | What it pays for |
| --- | --- |
| Namespace claim fee | Upfront Allocator escrow for a top-level namespace; [outcome-dependent settlement](/concepts/claiming-a-namespace) |
| Stellar fees and rent | Network transactions, contract resources and storage availability |
| Optional hosted subscription | Deployment-enabled operational services |

Current issuance contracts do not add a Soran fee to every name. A namespace owner may independently offer commercial or finite-term names under its own disclosed policy.

## Managed services

The implemented optional billing model can charge a yearly base floor plus a rate for names marked active by the hosted service. Prices and the USD/XLM conversion used for invoices are operator-set. A hosted live/cold flag is not proof of on-chain TTL status. Old chain-cost benchmarks shown in the console are not current network fee quotes.

Hosted services include platform-operated issuance/relay, branding, new webhook subscriptions and subscription-filtered directory reach. Existing webhook delivery and signing-secret rotation have different availability rules from creating new subscriptions.

## Pay an invoice

An invoice supplies the receiving account, exact native XLM amount and a unique hash memo. Send the full amount in one payment and preserve that memo. Invoice matching uses the memo; the payer need not be the namespace owner. Matching and UI updates depend on watcher health and are not guaranteed within a fixed time.

Use a fresh invoice if the old quote expired. Invoice payments are separate from a Resolver's name-payment instructions and from Allocator claim escrow.

## Ownership transfers and re-attestation

Hosted entitlement attaches to the current namespace owner. After a namespace transfer it can become `suspended_transfer`; the new owner re-attests in the console to resume eligible remaining service time. Paying another invoice does not itself lift that suspension. Other states include `active` and `lapsed`.

## Non-payment

A lapsed subscription can restrict hosted relays, branding, creation of new webhooks and directory reach. It does not revoke the on-chain namespace or alter a name's contract expiry. Self-custody contract operations remain available with the required signer and network fees.

On-chain resolution still depends on valid records, supported code and accessible storage. Do not interpret billing independence as a promise that archived entries or a failed RPC keep resolving. A timed namespace's own ownership expiry remains separate from the hosted subscription.
