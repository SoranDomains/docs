---
title: "Optional marketplace"
description: "Operator-custodied payment escrow and on-chain namespace transfers."
---

The marketplace is available **when enabled on a deployment**. It sells whole namespaces using operator-custodied payment escrow and Registry's two-step ownership transfer. It is separate from the Allocator's on-chain claim-fee escrow.

## Custody

The deployment's escrow account holds the buyer's money and seller's bond. The namespace stays with its owner until the proposed recipient accepts its Registry transfer. A displayed sale badge is hosted provenance derived from settlement records, not a new on-chain ownership token or guarantee.

## Seller flow

1. The namespace owner lists a price. Marketplace fees and bond amounts are operator-set and must be shown before commitment.
2. The seller funds the required performance bond with the exact supplied memo.
3. For a buyer, the seller proposes the **namespace Registry transfer** to that buyer's address.
4. After funded delivery is verified, the settlement service attempts payment to the seller and bond return under its rules.

Console admin/issuer access does not authorize listing as owner. A Name-view individual transfer is not the required namespace transfer. A listing with a funded deposit or delivery in progress cannot simply be withdrawn as if no sale existed.

## Buyer flow

Create an intent through the [market API](/api/market), check the live Registry proposal points to your wallet, then send the exact price and hash memo from a self-custody account. Finally, accept the namespace transfer from that wallet.

Do not pay a generic address returned by a name lookup in place of the exact escrow intent. Refunds go to the payment's source account; paying from an exchange can route a refund to its pooled account without a customer memo.

## Refund and bond policy

Seller cancellation, redirection or delivery elsewhere during a funded sale can entitle the buyer to a refund and seller-bond slash. If the buyer lets a standing offer expire without accepting, the refund policy does not treat that alone as seller breach. Successful delivery releases the price less fee and returns the eligible bond.

These are settlement rules implemented by the hosted operator. Payouts can be delayed or parked for review, including small amounts or uncertain chain state. They are not a guarantee of automatic delivery or a fixed refund time. Review the actual deposit/settlement status and [watcher health](/api/status).

A namespace remains transferable through its contract process without this optional marketplace. If hosted billing is enabled, a buyer may need to re-attest ownership for those services.
