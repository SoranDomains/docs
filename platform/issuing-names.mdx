---
title: "Issuing names"
description: "Set up your namespace and choose manual issuance or username claims in your app."
---

Once you own a namespace, you can issue names yourself or let users claim them in your app. First activate the namespace's Registrar and native Resolver, then choose how registration should work. The upfront namespace fee and any price you set for usernames are separate.

## Activate the namespace

1. Choose the Registrar policy, including reclaimability, transfers and the default name term.
2. Review the prepared deployment and sign with the namespace owner's wallet.
3. Deploy and configure the native Resolver to store payment and profile records.

An admin or issuer console role does not replace the owner's on-chain signature. If submission is interrupted, check its outcome before retrying.

A reclaimable policy lets the owner reclaim names under its rules. A zero-term, non-reclaimable policy gives names no expiry, but code and routing remain **unlocked** until the [permanence transition](/concepts/ownership-guarantees) succeeds.

## Automatic branded contract addresses

The console searches for a `C?SORAN...` address when preparing a new Registrar or native Resolver. Here `?` is Stellar's second address character: `A`, `B`, `C` or `D`.

You can follow progress or cancel before signing. Search time varies, and you can restart a canceled or failed search. The contract exists only after its deployment transaction is confirmed.

Address preparation computes candidate nonces locally without your signing key. Registry still verifies the owner and derives the [namespace-specific contract address](/concepts/names-and-namespaces#namespace-contract-addresses).

Individual names are records within these contracts. Existing contract addresses do not change. Direct callers may choose ordinary addresses, so verify Registry routing rather than a `SORAN` prefix.

## Issue names

| Mode | Who issues a name |
| --- | --- |
| Manual | The namespace owner authorizes issuance |
| Public | The user authorizes a claim under your settings; the owner can still assign explicitly reserved labels |

For manual or reserved issuance, supply a canonical label and valid holder address. The initial payment target is the holder. The holder can then publish a different address and optional memo through the Resolver. For Public mode, follow [automatic username claiming](/owner/automatic-claiming) to integrate registration into your app.

The console supports single issuance and CSV workflows with per-row outcomes. The Owner SDK supports batches of at most 23 names per transaction. Check confirmed outcomes and uncertain transaction hashes before retrying a row.

## Fees

You can make public username claims free or set a flat native XLM price paid directly to your G treasury. Choose a treasury that does not require a deposit memo. The contracts add no mandatory annual ownership charge or separate holder renewal fee. Stellar transaction fees and storage rent still apply; use the current transaction estimate.

When enabled on a deployment, managed billing can charge for hosted services. A namespace owner can also offer its own commercial service or timed names under its disclosed contract policy. These costs are distinct from the upfront top-level namespace claim fee.

## Dormancy, TTL and restoration

A hosted **cold** label means the service considers a name dormant. It does not establish on-chain archival or end ownership. A keeper can extend relevant contract and record TTLs while they are available.

Archived entries may need restoration before payment resolution succeeds. The hosted API does not substitute a database destination for a failed on-chain read. Cached profiles can show history, but payments need current instructions.

## Who can change what

| Signer | Authorized actions |
| --- | --- |
| Namespace owner | Claim settings, Manual/reserved issuance, permitted reclaim and owner renewal |
| Claimant | Self-service claim, with an eligibility proof only when the policy requires one |
| Holder | Name records, permitted individual transfer and eligible bounded renewal |
| Destination account | Its reverse and Primary display-name elections; the base G account signs for M routes |

Console team roles do not combine these separate authorities.
