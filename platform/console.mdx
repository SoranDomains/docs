The console at [soran.domains](https://soran.domains) provides namespace management, issuance, team access and webhooks. The native-claim release adds on-chain username registration controls; [release status](/reference/release-status) records its hosted cutover separately from the 6 September contract verification. Billing and marketplace features appear **when enabled on a deployment**; both are disabled on this testnet setup.

## Wallet sign-in

The console requests a short-lived, one-time wallet-signature challenge. It is designed for authentication and is not submitted as a network transaction. Verify the application and network context before signing. Authentication proves control of the signing account; namespace authorization is checked separately.

Your session includes namespaces you own on chain or operate through a console invitation. Switching namespaces re-scopes access. Signing out invalidates the server session.

## Operator roles

| Role | Console scope |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Team management and owner-only controls, with authority tied to the on-chain owner |
| Admin | Operational controls except team/permanence and other owner-bound actions |
| Issuer | Issuance-focused access; does not grant reclaim authority |

Console roles do not grant new smart-contract powers. On a self-custody namespace, issuance and namespace lifecycle operations still need the owner's signature. A holder's records and individual transfer need holder authority. Reverse/Primary for a destination require that destination's authorization.

## Custody modes

**Self-custody:** your wallet is the on-chain owner and signs prepared transactions. The server does not hold the signing authority for your namespace.

**Platform-operated:** the deployment's operating key owns/signs for the namespace, and permitted console actions use that signer. This is a different custody arrangement; inspect it before relying on a claim of wallet-only control.

## Common flows

- **Issuance:** prepare a branded Registrar address, authorize activation and issue names under its policy. [Address preparation](/platform/issuing-names#automatic-branded-contract-addresses) shows progress and supports cancellation before signing.
- **Names:** inspect current ownership, edit authorized records and manage individual name transfers.
- **Team:** invite/remove wallet-address operators.
- **Developers:** manage optional HTTP integrations and [webhooks](/platform/webhooks).
- **Billing/Market:** hosted commercial features when enabled.

The namespace claim flow displays the upfront XLM quote and refund terms, and checks its exact contract authorization before signing. Account/memo writes use an atomic native Resolver operation. The app's refund-credit control reads and prepares recovery of undelivered claim-fee payouts.

A transfer in the Names view is an **individual name transfer**. A whole namespace moves through Registry's separate proposal/acceptance process, described in [Owner lifecycle](/owner/lifecycle-and-transfers).

After an uncertain submission, inspect the transaction outcome before retrying. A successful simulation is not final inclusion.

## Username registration

Select your namespace and open **Username registration**. Connect the current
namespace owner wallet. Read the live contract state, then choose Manual or Public
mode, open/invitation/app-approved eligibility, username price and G treasury,
wallet quota and any explicit approval budgets. Configure and enable registration
before offering it in your own app signup. Console team membership does not replace
the owner’s on-chain signature.

Reserve administrative labels before opening claims. The reserved assignment flow
can issue them without exposing them through a temporary release. Public mode
blocks ordinary owner issuance even while claims are paused.

The maximum network fee defaults to 5 XLM and is separate from the username price.
Review the estimate before explicitly raising it for storage rent or other network
costs. A rejected estimate stops before wallet signing. After an interrupted
submission, use **Check transaction** before making another change; the saved hash
is a public recovery reference, not a private signing key.

Apps integrate through the [Owner and Holder SDKs](/owner/automatic-claiming); a
user is not required to visit the Soran console to claim during signup.
