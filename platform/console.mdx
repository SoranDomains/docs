Use the console at [soran.domains](https://soran.domains) to manage your namespace, issue names, configure username registration and invite your team. The current deployment runs on Stellar testnet. Billing and marketplace features appear **when enabled on a deployment**; both are disabled on this setup.

## Wallet sign-in

Connect your wallet and sign the short-lived, one-time sign-in challenge. It authenticates your account and is not submitted as a network transaction. Check the app and network shown in your wallet before signing.

You can then select a namespace you own on chain or operate through a console invitation. Switching namespaces changes your access scope. Signing out ends the server session.

## Operator roles

| Role | Console scope |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Team management and owner-only controls, with authority tied to the on-chain owner |
| Admin | Operational controls except team/permanence and other owner-bound actions |
| Issuer | Issuance-focused access; does not grant reclaim authority |

Console roles control access to the interface. Self-custody owner operations still require the owner's signature; holder record changes and individual transfers require holder authority. A destination account authorizes its own reverse and Primary elections.

## Custody modes

**Self-custody:** your wallet is the on-chain owner and signs prepared transactions. The server does not hold the signing authority for your namespace.

**Platform-operated:** the deployment's operating key owns and signs for the namespace. Permitted console actions use that signer. The mode depends on the on-chain owner key, not the namespace label.

## Common flows

| Console area | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Issuance** | [Activate your namespace and issue names](/platform/issuing-names) |
| **Username registration** | Set who can claim usernames in your app and what they pay |
| **Names** | Inspect ownership, edit authorized records and transfer individual names |
| **Team** | Invite or remove wallet-address operators |
| **Developers** | Manage HTTP integrations and [webhooks](/platform/webhooks) |
| **Billing / Market** | Use optional commercial features when enabled on a deployment |

The namespace claim flow shows the upfront fee and refund terms before signing. If a claim-fee payout could not be delivered, the refund-credit control lets you inspect and retry it. Payment record changes store the address and memo together in the native Resolver.

A transfer in the Names view is an **individual name transfer**. A whole namespace moves through Registry's separate proposal/acceptance process, described in [Owner lifecycle](/owner/lifecycle-and-transfers).

After an interrupted submission, check the transaction outcome before retrying. A successful simulation does not confirm the transaction.

## Username registration

1. Open **Username registration** and connect the current namespace owner's wallet.
2. Choose Manual or Public mode. For Public mode, choose open registration, invitations or app-approved eligibility.
3. Set the username price, receiving G treasury and wallet quota. App-approved registration also needs an approval account and explicit approval budgets.
4. Reserve administrative labels, review the live settings and enable registration.

Use reserved assignment to issue an administrative label while keeping it reserved. Public mode blocks ordinary owner issuance even while claims are paused.

The maximum network fee starts at 5 XLM per transaction, separate from the username price. This is a ceiling, not the amount charged. Review an estimate before raising the limit for storage rent or other network costs. If a submission is interrupted, use **Check transaction** before making another change.

Connect the [Owner and Holder SDKs](/owner/automatic-claiming) to your app's own signup or account settings. Your users can claim there without visiting the Soran console.
